From our workshop

Four steps from your measurements to your door.

The same team that builds for showrooms builds your order. You can visit the workshop and see your piece being made.

  1. 1

    Measure & draw

    Send us your room size and a photo you like. We send back a drawing with exact dimensions and a price.

  2. 2

    Frame

    Seasoned wood is cut and joined into a strong frame. This is the part you never see but always feel.

  3. 3

    Finish

    Upholstery, polish or lacquer in the fabric and shade you picked. We send photos before it leaves.

  4. 4

    Deliver & fit

    We deliver and set it up. Wardrobes and fitted pieces are installed on site.
